For §16 the tag is the evidence of precedence; this record archives it. Sections 1 to 15 are unchanged from version 1.4 (10.5281/zenodo.23964906).

Why §16 exists. Under the decision table of §15 the two registered judges landed in different rows on H4, the comparison of the revised criterion's units against the raw evidence of the same sessions on 242 LongMemEval s_cleaned instances: judge 1 showed a loss (−5.4 points, 95% interval [−10.3, −0.8]) and judge 2 showed non-inferiority at 10 points (−5.0, [−9.9, 0.0]). Both results were known when §16 was written, and §16 says so. To keep the new rule from being chosen after seeing the new judges, it fixes the judges, their gate and the aggregation before any of their calls on the data.

What §16 registers. Judge 3 is glm-5.3 (Z.ai) and judge 4 is gemini-4-argon, both with judge 2's prompt verbatim, grading the 484 existing rows; no answer is regenerated. Gate: each must reject all 16 smoke abstentions and accept the reference answer on at least 7 of 8 smoke instances. Primary: a committee label, correct when at least 3 of the 4 judges accept, a 2-2 tie counting as not correct, read with the §15 decision table and its margin of 10 points. Each judge is also reported alone. Two of the four judges share the reader's model family; §16 states this and does not correct it.
