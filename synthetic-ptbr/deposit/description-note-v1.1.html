A constructed corpus in Brazilian Portuguese (200 markdown files in the forms of an agent's working memory, for an invented publishing house; 45 planted facts and 5 false-premise questions), built so that the ground truth of every question is known by construction and kept apart from the labels an evaluator would record. With it, a short note that runs three of the five ruler failure mechanisms reported in Paper A (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24400444) against the published nox-mem package at two pinned versions, 3.5.2 and 3.11.1, one before and one after a change to how it ingests files.

Under the old ingest a gold reference keyed by chunk id was lost when its file was re-ingested unchanged or edited outside the evidence's section, while the evidence anchor survived; under the current ingest the id survived both, and in the one rename sequence run the id reached the evidence while the path-keyed anchor did not. Registered labels that omit some true positives changed the score of all 20 questions where they were incomplete, in both directions; no question scored zero. An ideal built from the ranking changed 5 of 45 scores.

Version 1.1 adds a second, pre-registered step: Paper A's LLM judges, with their model ids, prompts and request bodies, re-run on 555 question–chunk pairs and read against the planted key, which a blind reading of 100 pairs by the author contradicted once. The judges found every planted positive but one. Every judge accepted some of 15 planted decoys (chunks giving the asked attribute a value the text marks as rejected or superseded), from 7 to all 15, and these were half of all false acceptances. The judge Paper A used to check its gold accepted all 59 correct entries, none of 50 random chunks and 12 of the 15 decoys; the pooling judge accepted the same 12, and the two agreed with each other more than either agreed with the key. Version 1.0 is doi:10.5281/zenodo.24535848.

The archive holds the corpus, the seeded plan and its coverage table, one generation record per file, the frozen results for both versions, the step 2 pre-registration, amendment, blind human sample, runs, cost ledger and analysis, and the code with offline tests and the provenance gate. The corpus, plan and results are released under CC BY 4.0, the code under MIT. Not peer reviewed.
