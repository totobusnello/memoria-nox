# Paper D smoke, 2026-11-05: what the sample size depends on

Not a result. A smoke of the pipeline the pre-registration will freeze, on 5 production sessions,
to size the sample (decision D4). Counts only; questions, answers and units stay in a private
directory outside the repository.

## Pool and selection

`d-select.py` ran read-only on the host that keeps the transcript archive. Eligible: last event
from 2026-08-15 to 2026-10-29 (the freeze date of 2026-11-05 minus 7 days), not one of the 20
sessions spent on 2026-10-26/27, cleaned prose of at least 3,000 characters (the rule of
`audits/2026-10-26-replay-build.py`).

| prose floor | eligible sessions |
|---|---|
| 3,000 characters (used) | 639 |
| 6,000 | 490 |
| 10,000 | 237 |
| 20,000 | 50 |

The archive holds 1,726 sessions. Production sessions are short: only 50 eligible sessions reach
one full 20,000-character window.

A first selection used a floor of 25,000 characters, which has no precedent and left 25 eligible
sessions. Only the counts were seen; nothing was copied or read. It was discarded and the selection
rerun with the replay rule and the same seed (1105001). The 5 sessions were copied to the private
directory and hashed; they are excluded from the main draw.

## What it changes in the design

1. **C0 and C2 rarely disagree here.** 2 discordant pairs of 17 primary questions under judge 1.
   Paper 3's criterion effect at the full window was +24.8 to +27.3 pp on LongMemEval
   (`paper3-construction/CONFIRM-RESULT.md`); on these short sessions (3 to 6 distiller blocks) the
   sample has to be sized for a small effect, not for that one. Five sessions decide nothing about
   the effect.
2. **The ceiling gate depends on the judge.** Arm A reads 0.765 under judge 1 and 0.529 under judge
   2 on the same 17 questions; D7's 0.70 passes under one and fails under the other. The protocol
   has to name the judge the gate is read under.
3. **Sample size (D4), by McNemar on discordant pairs**, two-sided α 0.05, power 0.80, with a
   design effect of 1.5 for clustering by session (assumed, not measured):

| discordance | effect | primary questions | sessions at 3.4 per session |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.12 | 10 pp | 138 | 41 |
| 0.15 | 10 pp | 174 | 51 |
| 0.20 | 10 pp | 232 | 69 |
| 0.20 | 15 pp | 102 | 30 |

At US$ 0.0235 for 5 sessions, 80 sessions cost about US$ 0.40 in the ledger plus the writer.
