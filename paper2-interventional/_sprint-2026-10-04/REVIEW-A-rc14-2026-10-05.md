## Codex (verbatim)

1. Medium — Abstract and §8.1: the survey's metrics are mischaracterized. Quote: "classifies every metric in use as a score over a set of queries". Quote: "all three score the representation: given a set of queries, how well the system retrieves what is relevant." Problem: The survey explicitly includes end-to-end task success, issue resolution, memory integrity and false-memory metrics. These are not all retrieval scores. The repository's `RELATED-WORK.md` §2 also records this distinction. Survey §7.1, Table 3 (arxiv.org/html/2602.06052v4#S7.T3). Fix: "The survey covers retrieval, memory-quality, response-quality and end-to-end task metrics. Its metric taxonomy does not include a census of distinct memory items delivered under production traffic."
2. Medium — §5.6: the proposed falsifier does not follow from Proposition 1. Quote: "If an id enters from a stratum in which nobody left, the bonus crossed a stratum and Proposition 1 is false." Problem: Proposition 1 concerns pairwise ordering. Order-dependent deduplication can change selection across strata without changing any cross-stratum ordering. Executing the deposited comparator and `pickDedup` on a constructed example produced control {A,D} and treatment {B,C}, with strata A=B=0, C=1, D=2. Boosting B only reordered stratum 0; deduplication nevertheless admitted C and displaced D. This is the limitation already acknowledged in §5.3 (serving-brief.ts:420). Fix: "All 20 observed entries had an exit in the same stratum. This is an empirical property of these replay states. With order-dependent deduplication, an unmatched entry would not by itself refute Proposition 1." Replace the table's "Proposition 1 | survives" with "Same-stratum matching check | passes."
3. Medium — §5.1: severity independence is unsupported. Quote: "It is independent of the severity label". Problem: Production uses w × 0.043 × severity_pain; increasing w does not eliminate relative severity differences. Two designated candidates sharing a stratum can have different limiting orders under different labels (one slot, base 0.8/0.7, multipliers 0.5/0.25 keep the first; reversed multipliers select the second at w=100,000). The replay does not establish label independence (serving-brief-outcome.ts:359). Fix: "The ceiling was measured at `w = 100,000` with the recorded severity labels held fixed. Its sensitivity to changing those labels was not measured."
4. Medium — §5.5: Quote: "with eligibility held fixed, no additive salience adjustment could change selection there beyond the ceiling." Problem: holding eligibility fixed does not fix designation or relative bonuses; §5.7.1 and CEILING-DESIGNATION-SENSITIVITY-2026-08-28.json show 26/350 under unchanged eligibility vs 17/350. Fix: "With eligibility, designation and severity multipliers held fixed, the tested dose family saturated at 17/350 states. This is not a maximum over all additive salience adjustments."
5. Medium — §6: Quote: "the brief leads 245 to ≥151". Problem: 245 exceeding a lower bound of 151 does not establish that it exceeds the underlying quantity; superficie-de-exposicao.py gets the search figure from last_accessed_at (tracked-search candidacy, §3.1). Fix (table cell): "The common-window artifact reports 245 live curated entities with brief records and 151 with `last_accessed_at ≥ 2026-06-04`. These different record predicates do not establish which surface delivered more distinct entities; the earlier cumulative reading of 617 has no preserved field."
6. Medium — §5.7.1: Quote: "`churn` is the symmetric difference of two sets". Problem: diffBriefs defines churn = wouldEnter.length; for equally sized sets this is half the symmetric difference; the 20 displacement events would be 40 under the stated definition (serving-brief-diversity.ts:159). Fix: "A shared tie-break does not guarantee cancellation: it can change membership at the selection boundaries. Here `churn` counts treated-only ids; for equally sized sets, it is half the cardinality of their symmetric difference."
7. Medium — Abstract, §1, §4.1.1 and §9: Quotes: "serving uniformly would be useless"; "serving memory at random would be worse than not serving it"; "serving uniformly would destroy its value." Problem: downstream utility effects asserted while §4.5 says utility was not measured. Fix: "Uniform serving is used only as a capacity reference; its effect on agent utility was not measured."
