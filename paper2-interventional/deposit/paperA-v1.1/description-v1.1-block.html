What changed in v1.1 (2026-10-05)

The text above is the v1.0 description with its two dated errata, translated from the Portuguese published with v1.0; content and numbers are unchanged, and the Portuguese original stays on the v1.0 record. Version 1.1 is the manuscript translated to English and revised: the 2026-09-21 corrections, a 2026-10-04 audit of claims that had aged, and the reviews of 2026-10-05. Every change is recorded, with its source, in Appendix F of the manuscript; the 2026-10-05 round is Appendix F-5. Where the v1.0 description and v1.1 differ, v1.1 holds.

Title. v1.0 was titled "Spare capacity, narrow surface: what a production agent-memory system actually surfaces". v1.1 is titled "Spare capacity, narrow surface: the exposure record of a production agent-memory system": the old ending used "actually" as an intensifier and repeated surface/surfaces. The title note at the top of the manuscript records the change.

Recomputed with production code. Three quantities were recomputed rather than relabelled, each with a dated artifact.

  • Access counterfactual (§4.3.2). The published 2/3/5 → 131/129/128 came from a script whose recency and importance default are not those of the production function. With calculateSalience the three chunks rank 1, 3 and 4 by salience among the 149 served chunks and fall to a tie at ranks 44–46 when the access component is zeroed (23–47 across instants and access-timing assumptions). Dedup was not replayed, so these ranks do not by themselves establish final served membership. "Beyond rank 100" and "exactly 52 places lower" are withdrawn. The claim that zeroing access takes the three out of the top-10 stands. out/SALIENCE-COUNTERFACTUAL-PROD-2026-10-05.json.
  • Tie-break exposure (§5.7.1). Recounted on the comparator's key instead of the SQL pre-rank expression: 34–42 pairs at second resolution (0.59–0.73%) and 1,197 at day resolution. The earlier 68 / 269 / 917 / 1,656 remain cited as the pre-rank count. out/TIEBREAK-EXPOSURE-PROD-2026-10-05.json.
  • Bonus against step (§4.4, §5.4). The 0.0946 and 1.79× used a severity multiplier of 0.5 that only the replay's summary applies; production applies 0.25. The bonus is 0.0473, 0.90× the largest adjacent gap, and the claim that the items crossed several positions is withdrawn. out/BONUS-VS-STEP-2026-10-05.json.

Claims corrected or narrowed. Several sentences of the v1.0 description above are superseded:

  • "never exposed 83.78% of its own corpus": the 56,288 live chunks (83.78%) have neither a brief-log record nor a positive search counter. That is a lower bound on non-delivery by the brief and by tracked search, not a measurement that they were never exposed; non-delivery across all agent-facing search is not established.
  • "showed 1,787 distinct chunks: 2.66%": the brief delivered 1,635 distinct live chunks (2.43%); 1,787 counts the 152 that were served and later deleted.
  • "search is initiated by the agent and accounts for most of the recorded exposure": the search counter records that a chunk was a top candidate of some tracked call, whoever initiated it (automated callers included). It bounds from above what tracked search returned and identifies no initiator.
  • "the main one is determined by search traffic from months ago and does not respond to score adjustment": the three chunks present in every brief of the week hold ranks 1, 3 and 4 by salience among the 149 served only through tracked search traffic from months ago (44–46 with access zeroed). The high-pain pin is the other condition: it protects only what the score has already placed in the brief, and it is measured as necessary for one of the three (lifting it removes chunk 116107 from every reconstructed brief; the other two stay). The main pool would respond to a score adjustment, and nobody adjusts it.
  • "exhausts 100% of an eligible pool of 108 chunks every day": measured on 2026-08-26 to 08-29, inside a period of defective session ingestion, and describing that regime. The coverage channel's daily reach is bounded by its eligible population (path patterns, the importance/pain floor and age windows); within a brief it responds to score only within last_served ties (17/350 in the replay) and then saturates.
  • "eight are above 32.5%": seven are above 32.5% and one is at 32.5%.

Also narrowed: in the derivation of §5 the "if and only if" entry condition now holds only when the item is the only bonused one of its stratum, the closed formula for the saturating bonus is withdrawn, and the generality claims are stated for rankers that serve a prefix of the lexicographic order. Numbers in the text that have no preserved artifact are flagged where they appear.
