Version 2.0 (2026-10): the trial report

Versions 1.0 to 1.12 of this record are the pre-registration of a randomized trial of memory dosing in a production agent fleet, and its amendments. Version 2.0 is the report of that trial: the manuscript A registered horizon that outlived its intervention: a pre-registered randomized trial of memory dosing in a production agent fleet (registered-horizon-outlived-intervention-v2.0.md, and the same text as PDF), with the artifacts its numbers come from. This version is also the grouped amendment of the registration: in one record it declares every deviation from the deposited registration (Appendix A of the manuscript; listed below) and reports the result. The registration files stay where they were deposited, OSF yf7d2 (2026-08-18) and Zenodo v1.12, 10.5281/zenodo.22110203 (2026-08-26), and are not copied into this version. The surface measurements of the same system are reported separately (Paper A, 10.5281/zenodo.23163119).

Result. Under the registered analysis no hypothesis rejects. The primary outcome reported, H1c (the share of opportunities that yield a repeated failure), is null: the registered re-randomization test gives p = 0.4006, and the estimate is −0.0140 (95% BCa interval −0.0464 to +0.0110, 19 epochs, 11 treatment and 8 control). H1, the primary named by v1.12, gives p = 0.3294 tested alone, and every Holm-adjusted p-value in the H1a–H1c and H2 family is 1.0. No interval of the H1 family excludes zero. Two H2 intervals (cost) exclude zero in the direction of a costlier treatment under the BCa construction, and the registered H2 test does not reject. The null does not show that the mechanism has no effect: with 19 epochs, no reduction from the planning baseline, including total elimination, reached 80% power.

Why the trial was short. The registration set a horizon of 234 daily epochs. The 19 items designated for dosing all left the candidate pool together on 2026-09-20, after 20 epochs; past that point no dose reaches them, and treatment and control are the same intervention. The designation was chosen on 2026-08-26, after v1.12 was deposited, and was not deposited. The trial was closed before its registered horizon; 20 epochs ran and 19 form the analysis set.

Deviations from the deposited registration, reported in the manuscript (§1, Appendix A):

  • Primary outcome: v1.12 names H1 (repeated-failure density per session-hour); the paper reports H1c as primary, a switch decided on 2026-08-30, before the assignment seed was drawn, in documents that were not deposited. H1 is reported beside it under both readings.
  • Horizon: 234 epochs registered, 20 realized, because of the designation above.
  • Stopping rule: a rule written before the seed (close the arm with a null if Epoch-1 coverage fell below 36.7%) was found only after the trial; no contemporaneous record shows it was evaluated, and its condition was met under one reading and not under another.
  • Assignment rule: the seed declaration named a rule other than the registered assign_arms.py and retracted it in writing 24 min 38 s after the drand round, before Epoch 1; run offline, the two rules differ in 11 of the 20 realized epochs.
  • Adjudication: the registration locked a census of the live study; the analysis adjudicates the error stratum in full and a hash-ordered sample of 800 of the other 5,556 episodes (weight 6.945). An attempt to restore the census after unblinding stopped at a test-retest gate declared before it started.
  • Analysis specification: written on 2026-09-10, before any outcome was computed, and not deposited. The analysis reported combines it with the deposited provisions and with a panel-substitution rule declared after the trial; the analysis of earlier drafts is kept as a sensitivity analysis.

Sham replays, an instrument check. The dosing code was replayed over the served briefs with the real designation and with 20 sham designations matched to it. The real designation changed more brief states than every sham: 132 against 81–122 on the 2,646 states of the w = 4 epochs, and 790 against 449–583 on the 11,812 reconstructible states of the hash-verified trial window, p = 1/21 in both, which is the floor for 20 shams. The second replay contains the states of the first, so it is not a replication. This shows that the dose acted on what was served. It says nothing about outcomes, and the rank is not a calibrated randomization p-value.

Files. artifacts-v2.0.zip holds the registered-analysis outputs (out/ITT-REGISTRADO-*.json, v1 to v4), the results notes and frozen copies of the estimator (B-registered/), the adjudication census record without its raw episode files (B-censo/), the sham replays (summaries, determinism checks and the two runs archives), the figures, the review receipts and verdicts, the per-round records of how each review finding was checked and applied, and the trial's own outputs kept outside the repository (_ballast/, with the hash manifest that covers them). scripts-v2.0.zip holds the measurement and check scripts and the PDF build. _derived/panel-verdicts/, also in artifacts-v2.0.zip, holds the panel's verdict on each adjudicated episode, from the two verdict files the manuscript cites: episode id, panelist, model requested and model served, verdict and severity level, call status. The panelists' free-text reasons are removed, because they describe the episodes. MANIFEST-v2.0.json lists the SHA-256 of every packaged file, which copies were redacted (local paths and host names only), every path the manuscript cites and where it is found, and what was left out and why. Episode text, the panelists' free-text reasons and the production databases are not included.
