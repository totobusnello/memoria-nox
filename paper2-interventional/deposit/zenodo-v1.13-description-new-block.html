VERSION 1.13 (2026-08-27) — the dose scale is invalidated and the study is BLOCKED. This version adds AMENDMENT-v1.13.md, which withdraws Δ_cut = 0.043 from the status of a scientific parameter and blocks the start of the study until a prospective calibration protocol is registered. The block is a decision of the amendment, not an external impediment.

The deductive core. The coverage-pool comparator is lexicographic: when last_served differs it returns al − bl and never consults salience. The Paper 2 boost is additive in salience — the subordinate coordinate. So the referent the registration names for Δ_cut (“the measured salience spread at the brief cut”) does not exist: the code applies no threshold at all. A quantity analogous to it does exist — the salience gap within strata of identical last_served — but no functional of its distribution was registered, validated for transportability, or frozen before calibration. The band {2.0, 4.0, 7.5} therefore falls as a calibrated scale. Absolute doses continue to exist as executable constants, and prospective recalibration remains open; what dies is the spread-relative interpretation.

What is closed. The designation rule that v1.12 declared an open defect. It was replaced by a draw with a publicly declared seed: drand quicknet round 31657512 (emitted 20:25:00Z), declaration pushed at 20:07:24Z — 1,056 seconds of verifiable precedence, with the round returning HTTP 425 at the time of writing. The 55-row verdict frame was deposited before the randomness existed. An independent reviewer reproduced the entire chain from the public beacon plus the deposited CSV, obtaining the same 19 designated chunks and the same set hash.

Fifteen new retractions (29–43), and seven of them retract this amendment's own earlier redactions. Among them: the auto-extinction hypothesis was not tested (the reconstructed series is entirely prior to treatment) rather than refuted; there were five verification probes writing 25 rows, not three writing 15 — two of them fired 55 seconds after the mechanism went live, contaminating the first minute of the very window whose rate is reported; the script that claimed to decontaminate performed a temporal rollback, removing 3,735 rows to exclude 25; a rate was published from a window left open at the top (11/310, which had aged to 359 rows twelve hours later); and a z-test against zero was removed, zero being the boundary of the binomial space.

Instrument remediation. REMEDIATION-2026-08-27.md records three defects in the measurement instruments and what re-measuring changed. Two corrections that the adversarial round led the author to propose turned out to be wrong, and the draft right: they came from the rollback rather than from probe exclusion. The lesson is registered as a requirement of the prospective protocol — reproduce a published anchor before varying anything, because a reconstruction that disagrees with a published number may disagree by definition rather than by drift.

Six adversarial reviews, five distinct training families, all with verifiable receipts. Two reviewed the first redaction and both recommended against depositing it; two of them independently found the same central contradiction in the second. The sixth verified from the outside — recomputing every statistic, checking every sha256, opening every cited line in the deposited blobs — and found a defect five readings had missed: the document claimed to hold the outputs of five voices when it holds one.

What this version deposits that earlier ones did not. The serving code at the commit this amendment describes, pinned by sha256 of file bytes rather than by commit alone — earlier redactions cited a commit hash that does not exist, its name having been rewritten by a history reconciliation while the content survived. The unit test file, without which the claim that five source mutations made the tests fail is unfalsifiable. The nineteen measurement scripts, three of them marked as records of error. The closed-window serving log, which makes the central rate recomputable from the package rather than from the author's assertion. And the adversarial receipts, with an explicit declaration of which outputs did not survive and are therefore unverifiable by a third party.

What remains open, stated plainly. No script here performs a full replay of the serving pipeline, so what is measured is ordering, not selection, and the definition of opportunity that follows is approximate by construction. Nothing here is randomised: every comparison is before/after, so none identifies a causal effect. T_seed_assign remains undeclared, now for a stronger reason — one does not draw arms for an invalidated scale.
