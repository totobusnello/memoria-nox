## Honest framing

MuSiQue is specifically designed to defeat single-shot RAG by requiring 2-4
sequential hops where shortcut answers fail.

**This baseline is single-shot retrieval + answer generation** (nox-mem hybrid
search → top-K chunks → gpt-4.1-mini generates the final answer in one pass).
We do NOT yet have Q3 Iterative Retrieval (multi-step planner that re-queries
based on intermediate hops), which is the architecture that closes the gap
to IRCoT-class results (35-40% F1) on this benchmark.

Headline framing (to be filled post-run):
> nox-mem **single-shot** retrieval baseline on MuSiQue: **TBD% answer F1**.
> Q3 Iterative Retrieval (planned, Lab Q1 2026) predicted to close ~50% of
> the gap to specialized iterative SOTA (IRCoT 35.8% F1) ⇒ projected ~30-35% F1
> with future Q3 phase.

If single-shot baseline is in the 20-30% range:
- COMPETITIVE with Standard RAG GPT-3/3.5 baselines (16-22%) and Self-Ask (15%).
- BELOW IRCoT (35.8%) — expected; iterative is the architecture for this bench.
- BELOW paper's trained supervised models (42-50%) — expected; they're trained
  on MuSiQue train set, we're zero-shot RAG.

If single-shot baseline >30%:
- ABOVE Standard RAG baselines: nox-mem retrieval foundation is strong.
- COMPETITIVE with IRCoT in the single-shot regime — would be a notable result
  given iterative methods burn 3-5× more compute.
